# The Root of the Fruits

We often hear terms like **cloud computing**, **computer science**, and **computer engineering**, yet we rarely stop to think about the word they all have in common.

According to the **Association for Computing Machinery (ACM)**, **computing** is the study of algorithms and systems designed to manipulate, store, transmit, and protect digital information.

Historically associated with calculation and counting, computing has evolved from mechanical machines to digital electronic systems, becoming one of the fundamental sciences behind technological innovation, process automation, and the miniaturization of modern devices.

*Interesting... but...*

Hold on.

If we look deeper, we find that the word **computing** originates from the Latin *computatio*, derived from *computare*, meaning **to calculate**.

Now imagine that we defined **1 + 1 = 3** as a rule. Does that make sense? Of course not. However, if we programmed a calculator with that rule, it would faithfully return the same result because it simply follows the instructions it was given.

This is the essence of computing. It is not merely about performing calculations; it is about defining rules, interpreting information, and transforming it into meaningful results. The way data is received, processed, and converted into knowledge depends on the logic we design and the physical systems that execute it.

In other words, computing is the art of transforming information into solutions. Calculations are only the roots; the technologies we use every day are the fruits.
